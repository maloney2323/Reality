# Reality: continuous business outcome and self-improvement contract

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Scope:** Architecture acceptance target, NOT a claim of deployed capability.  
**Existing foundation:** Signal Cleaner, verified Universe / Continuity, user-goal projection, discovered work identity, authorized action policy, DB-atomic single-use reservation, execution engine, independent verification, constitutional learning bridge, self-evolution and self-build candidates.

## Product purpose

Reality should discover real work for a customer **without requiring them to know every task beforehand**; keep their user-authored goals and constraints authoritative; complete a task inside an exact bounded authority; independently verify outcomes; calculate evidence-backed value; discover what should change about the customer's workflow or Reality's own capabilities; test candidate improvements and return to the SAME customer objective. Continuous self-improvement is part of the target product, not merely a dashboard add-on.

Never turn an assistant proposal into the user's objective; never equate a tool call with verified completion; never equate a successful test with authorization to deploy an upgrade.

## One full-loop work identity

Use a stable `work_id` for the whole cycle. Store, through the existing Continuity lineage:

1. **Observation and opportunity:** business/world/account scope; immutable source observations and their time; provenance; discovery method; why work may matter.
2. **User authority:** original authenticated user goal, explicit goal transition receipt, required permissions, the difference between the customer's goal and Reality's proposed subwork.
3. **Plan and baseline:** exact deliverables, constraints, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria; known/unknown human time, role, error/rework and cost baseline **with sources**. Missing baseline is UNKNOWN, not zero.
4. **Bounded authorization:** exact work fingerprint, target connector, user approval and expiry, current-goal epoch, one-time reservation; separate upgrade/promotion authority.
5. **Execution:** provider request, idempotency handle if available, provider execution ID, observed attempt and material uncertainties; no blind retries after unknown external outcome.
6. **Outcome and reconciliation:** independent, fresh provider or customer-owned readback; compare intended vs observed outcome; record verified / unresolved / failed with evidence and timeline, never model confidence as proof.
7. **Economic outcome:** hours avoided vs hours still spent; oversight/approval/rework; actual AI/connector expenses; quality, revenue and customer effects; capacity freed vs true payroll reduction vs realized cash savings. Record the **measurement method and evidence**. Projections and estimated labor value must be labeled and separated.
8. **Improvement opportunity:** pattern across verified work, source-linked limitation, estimated opportunity and uncertainty, scope (customer's workflow vs Reality's software/model prompts), expected success condition.
9. **Candidate experiment:** test plan and controlled sandbox, frozen baseline and independently graded outcome, failure cases and rollback plan. A proposal is never an already-installed capability.
10. **Promotion and continuation:** separate explicit permission to roll out consequential customer or software changes; independently verify the upgrade changed results; return to original `work_id`/goal (or open a source-linked child task) and update the next plan.

## Non-negotiable gates

- Model is free to suggest/propose; owner remains authoritative for goals and consequential change.
- Every status is observable: discovered, planned, awaiting goal, awaiting approval, reserved, dispatching, uncertain, provider-verified, reconciled, learning-proposed, experiment-verified, promotion-awaiting-approval, promoted, continuation.
- The same identity and provenance are reconstructible after restarts and browser refreshes, even when no customer is actively prompting Reality.
- Persist failure and unknown states as honestly as successes.
- A business job is only counted as completed after independent outcome verification.
- Self-improvement is only a success if the tested upgrade changes a measured outcome on a future task, and the customer objective remains intact.
- Return on investment is not a substitute for quality/reliability and must not be manufactured from speculative labor hours.
- Do not re-enable deprecated direct public write routes or promote PR #140 until the corresponding signed-in, safe preview proofs pass.
