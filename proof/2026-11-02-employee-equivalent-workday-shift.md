# Reality employee-workday compression proof — first source-backed shift

Objective: prove that Reality performs useful *completed* business maintenance
work, detects missing capabilities and corrections, and reduces hands-on
human effort versus a source-backed human labor baseline.

Do NOT count:
- review-only investigations as fixed bugs;
- GitHub issue/PR summaries as completed customer business jobs;
- passing model tests as installed verified capabilities;
- timestamp-based waiting time as human labor;
- hypothetical wage calculations as realized customer cash savings.

For this first phase, execute the existing 12 real source-specific
business-work deliverables and the independent model candidate research,
including blind offline verification and immutable provider receipts.

**A successful first phase is partial research-and-build evidence only.**
Exact owner approvals, fresh original-work attachment, isolated activation,
same-job resumption, and independent final settlement are still required
for a full autonomous shift. No production changes or owner approval bypass.
No fabricated 8-hour labor baseline.

## Future full-shift acceptance requirements

- 480 minutes of *defensibly documented comparable human effort* across
  a deduplicated set of work items, not made-up averages;
- multiple independent customer jobs end-to-end and their original work IDs
  resolved through settlement receipts;
- at least one genuine new missing capability self-generated and corrected;
- independent sandbox and post-install regression evidence;
- owner approves the exact consequential step just once for each artifact,
  without transfer or replay;
- measured end-to-end wall-clock time, model/infra costs, human approval and
  review minutes, interruption and recovery;
- actual retained progress and no duplicate effects across interruption;
- an independently reconciled net economic value or explicitly UNKNOWN.

A scorecard must distinguish:
attempted, researched, built, verified, installed, resumed, and
independently settled. No automatic claim of a completed shift.
